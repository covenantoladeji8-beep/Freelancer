Love your job

People work with us for different reasons - not just the usual tech company perks. People work with us for the challenge, our scale, our open-source technology, the push to constantly up-skill, and the direct impact you will have on our business and its products.

This won't be your typical cog-in-the-machine type of job. You will be exposed to the fast pace of a rapidly growing global technology company. If you're a high achiever with talent, looking for something more than a boring job in corporate, want to work with the best and brightest, don't need to be handheld, and want to be in at ground zero with a company that has a shot at being one of the biggest sites on the Internet, then please join us.

Why Freelancer?

We are on a mission to redefine the future of work. With over 80 million registered users, Freelancer.com is the world's largest freelancing and crowdsourcing marketplace. We work on cutting-edge technology, solve complex distributed scaling problems, and empower individuals worldwide to make a living.

What We Look For

  • Strong problem solvers with deep technical mastery and high ownership
  • Passion for meritocracy, speed, and continuous improvement
  • Ability to write clean, maintainable code or build exceptional products
  • Data-driven mindset with a bias toward measurable impact

Our Benefits

Competitive remuneration, equity packages, fully catered lunches, state-of-the-art workstations, quarterly hackathons, global relocation possibilities, and comprehensive health coverage.
