Freelancer Limited

Freelancer is the world's largest freelancing and crowdsourcing marketplace by number of users and projects. Freelancer was founded in 2009 and is listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX: FLN) and OTCQX Best Markets in the United States (OTCQX: FRLCY).

Investing from the United States

Freelancer Limited has established a sponsored Level 1 American Depositary Receipt (ADR) program in the United States. ADRs trade over-the-counter on the OTCQX Best Market under the ticker symbol FRLCY. Each Freelancer Limited ADR represents five (5) ordinary shares in Freelancer Limited.

The Bank of New York Mellon acts as depositary bank for the ADR program. BNY Mellon's ADR division provides comprehensive information for investors at www.adrbnymellon.com.

Investing From Australia

You can buy Freelancer shares through a broker that supports shares on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX). Freelancer's ticker is ASX: FLN. You should seek advice from a financial services professional before investing.

Note: ASX has a rule called the Minimum Marketable Parcel (MMP). For any first-time purchase of a given ASX stock, you must buy a "marketable parcel" worth at least A$500 before brokerage fees.

Investing From Other Countries

Freelancer shares can generally be purchased through international brokers that support trading on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) or the US OTCQX market. Interactive Brokers is an online broker that takes registrations from residents of many countries around the world and supports shares traded on the ASX.

Managing Your Shares

Freelancer Limited's share register is maintained by Boardroom Pty Limited. Shareholders can manage their holdings, update banking details, elect communications preferences, and view dividend statements online via the Boardroom InvestorServe portal at www.investorserve.com.au.

Shareholder FAQ

How can I invest?

You can buy Freelancer shares through a broker that supports shares on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX). Freelancer's ticker is ASX: FLN. You should seek advice from a financial services professional before investing.

Note: ASX has a rule called the Minimum Marketable Parcel (MMP). For any first-time purchase of a given ASX stock, you must buy a "marketable parcel" worth at least A$500 before brokerage fees.

How do I find a stockbroker?

A full list of Australian stockbrokers and other information about buying or selling shares can be found on the ASX website. Freelancer shares can generally only be purchased through an ASX registered broker, so if you are not a resident of Australia you may need to seek alternative arrangements. Interactive Brokers is an online broker that takes registrations from residents of many countries around the world that supports shares traded on the ASX.

Do Freelancer.com users who are also Freelancer shareholders get any extra benefits?

Yes! The Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program offers shareholders of Freelancer Limited (ASX: FLN) certain benefits including reduced fees on a range of Freelancer services and exclusive support.

Where can I find company announcements?

You can find company announcements on the ASX company page for Freelancer Limited, or subscribe above to receive our announcements via email.

When will Freelancer make company announcements that may affect my shares?

Freelancer makes announcements in accordance with the continuous disclosure requirements of the ASX Listing Rules and Corporations Act. Key reporting dates (half year, full year, and AGM) are published in advance on the investor calendar.

Where do I find historical share price information?

Historical share price charts and market data can be accessed on the Australian Securities Exchange website (asx.com.au) under ticker FLN, or via financial data providers including Bloomberg, Reuters, Google Finance, and Yahoo Finance.

How can I check my FLN holding?

You can view and manage your shareholding online through our share registry, Boardroom Pty Limited, by registering for an InvestorServe account at www.investorserve.com.au.

How do I update my shareholder information?

Changes of address, bank account details, TFN/ABN notifications, and communication preferences can be completed online via Boardroom InvestorServe, or by submitting written forms directly to Boardroom Pty Limited.

How do I find contact details for Freelancer's share registry?

Boardroom Pty Limited contact details are located in the sidebar of this Investor Centre, or by calling 1300 737 760 (within Australia) or +61 2 9290 9600 (International).

The Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program

The Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program offers shareholders of Freelancer Limited (ASX: FLN)(OTCQX: FRLCY) certain benefits including reduced fees on a range of Freelancer services.

Benefits at a Glance

Fee Rebates on Freelancer.com

A rebate of 5% of project and contest commissions will be credited to your Freelancer.com account, calculated monthly, for each full calendar month that you remain eligible in the program.

Priority Customer Support

You get priority customer support from our 24x7x365 multilingual support team.

Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Badge

A unique badge will appear on your Freelancer.com and WarriorForum profiles, signifying you are a shareholder of the company.

Access Your Benefits

These benefits are available to individuals with a minimum of 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares. Simply click the button below and fill out the application form to start accessing your benefits.

Where to Purchase Shares

The ticker symbol for Freelancer is ASX: FLN for anyone purchasing shares from Australia.

Note: Under ASX rules called 'Minimum Marketable Parcel' (MMP), your first purchase of FLN (or any ASX-listed company) must be at least A$500 worth of shares (excluding brokerage fees), regardless of which broker you use.

Terms and Conditions

To be eligible for the Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program, you must meet the following:

  • A shareholder must be a validly registered user of either the Freelancer.com or the Warrior Forum website (Freelancer User). Suspended accounts will not be eligible. Freelancer Users who breach the Terms of Use of either website may have their Program membership suspended or cancelled, at the discretion of Freelancer Limited.
  • A shareholder must be a natural person(s) and:
    • owns 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares; or
    • is nominated by a company, trust or incorporated body that owns 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares. A company, trust or incorporated body can nominate one natural person to receive the full benefits of the package.
  • Shareholders must provide proof of share ownership via a holding statement or certified statement from their broker.
