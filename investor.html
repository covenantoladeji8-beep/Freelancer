Freelancer Limited

Freelancer is the world's largest freelancing and crowdsourcing marketplace by number of users and projects. Freelancer was founded in 2009 and is listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX: FLN) and OTCQX Best Markets in the United States (OTCQX: FRLCY).
